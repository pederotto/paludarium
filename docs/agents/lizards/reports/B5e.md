# B5e report: real pump logic for every filter (feat/lizards, one commit "B5e: ...", files by path)
**Result:** every filter pump has real figures. `PUMPS` (equipment.js) is the ladder (Tern / Heron / Crane / Stork / Pelican, one maker family). sim/filterflow.js `pumpFit` gives sponge, corner foam and canister the smallest pump with lift <= 0.8 x its max head that meets the filter's flow, sets its knob to that flow, and flags the largest pump if none does. Bed, hang-on-back and internal keep the size bought, with `need` naming the size the tank needs. Curve: the centrifugal parabola H = Hmax (1 - (Q/Qmax)^2) (`filterPumpCurve`). Lift: the open sponge box lifts from its own water to an outlet 1.5 cm over the pool. The sealed canister is a closed loop: its static lift is 1.5 cm, and both hoses add friction. In-tank pumps lift from their water to their outlet; the hang-on-back lifts up to its box. Re-fitted per half cm of level (memoised), not per frame. Target: the existing per-kind `F.lph` sizes the pump; `TURNOVER` = 4 x the pool's litres an hour sizes the bought filters, and fitted ones get a "small" note in Care. Hang-on-back max head 0.4 m and internal 0.55 m are estimates with no sheet; Pelican's bore of 25 mm is also an estimate (all marked in FILTER_SHEETS.md). The rated flow of the 9 bought rows was recalibrated to the new curve (hang-on-backs at a pool 6 cm under the rim). Care names the fitted pump, its head and the lift. It shows a red note when the water is too low (the flow is 0, the head is never raised) and an amber note when undersized, naming the size needed.
