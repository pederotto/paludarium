# B5e proposal: real pumps for every filter (step 1, analysis only)
1. **Ladder as data** (new `PUMPS` in content/equipment.js; figures = FILTER_SHEETS rows, invented names): Tern 300 L/h / 60 cm / 7 W / 12 mm, adj. 170-300; Heron 600 / 100 / 7 / 12, adj. 250-600; Crane 1000 / 140 / 15 / 16, adj. 400-1000; Stork 2100 / 240 / 38 / 19, adj. 1400-2100; Pelican 3000 / 270 / 55 / bore not found, adj. 1800-3000 (all one fetched maker family, rows added to the sheet). Each row: `{ id, name, lph, hmax, watts, bore, adj, sheet }`.
2. **Curve:** the centrifugal pump's parabola H = Hmax (1 - (Q/Qmax)^2), i.e. Q = Qmax sqrt(1 - H/Hmax) (shut-off head at zero flow, head falling with the square of the flow). New `filterPumpCurve` in sim/filterflow.js; hydro.js `pumpCurve` (Q = Qmax (1 - (H/Hmax)^2), the opposite bend) stays for the main pump (test :35). No sheet has a 2nd curve point (all "not found"), so the exponent stays 2; a fetched point would replace it.
3. **Regulator:** these pumps have a real flow knob. It is set once, at the reference pool (12 cm, where `F.lph` is already calibrated), to the valve head k Q^2 that puts the clean working point on `F.lph`; after that the flow moves with lift and clog as now (test :20 keeps its meaning). The knob never adds head the pump lacks.
4. **Need per installation:** static lift = `MOUNTS[mount].lift` unchanged (cabinet: level + 70.8 - FILTER_TOP; pool 1.5; rim TANK.h - level + 4; internal 1; bed level + 1.5 - plenum line); hose friction = existing `hoseLoss` (Darcy-Weisbach, laminar/Blasius, k 1.5) on the mount's bore and length; media head at the target. Target flow = the kind's rated `F.lph`; water volume enters as fit: pool litres outside the kind's `tank` range (new kinds) or above 4x `F.lph` per hour (old kinds, guess at a keeper's turnover rule) is a Care note "too small for N L".
5. **Design levels:** 12 cm and the deepest pool, 0.5 x TANK.h (guess: no level cap found in src). Worst case wins.
6. **Selection** `pumpFor(kind, tank)` (pure, memoised on TANK): smallest rung with worst lift <= 0.8 Hmax AND Q(lift + media + hose at target) >= target. None: the largest rung, flagged `undersized { lift, need }`; its flow is whatever the curve gives (0 when lift >= Hmax). Hmax is never raised.
7. **Who gets which:** sponge, matten, canister (pump not a player choice): fitted from the ladder. Bed (Tern/Heron/Crane are the products): the bought rung is kept, `pumpFor` names the size needed and Care flags a smaller one. Hang-on-back and internal (pump built into the product): the family's 3 sizes are their ladder, same flag ("needs Birch or larger").
8. **Expected fits (worked by hand, not run):** sponge 12 cm lift 67.8 -> Heron, at 45 cm lift 100.8 -> Crane (Q 380 >= 120); canister 52.8 -> Crane, 85.8 -> Crane (Q 440 >= 400, thin); matten -> Tern; bed S/M/L lift < 6 -> Tern fits all. Stork/Pelican only for deeper pools than 0.5 h.
9. **Old figures that go:** sponge {190, 145}, matten {150, 80}, canister {560, 150} (none on a sheet). HOB hmax 120 and internal 55 are modelled, sheet "not found": step 2 writes them into the sheet as "modelled" so the figure check has a row; a real HOB head is unknown and likely far lower (tall tank lift 82 cm passes 0.8 x 120 = 96 only because of the modelled figure).
10. **Tests that change:** :10 (rated flow vs `F.pump.lph`: pump becomes a rung lookup), :30 (working point on `pumpCurve(head, F.pump.hmax)`: new curve, valve head in head, chosen rung), :35 stays, :169/:180 (bed pump literals [[300,60,7]...]: compare to PUMPS/sheet instead), :175 (`rows[i].pump.lph` monotone: read through the rung). :20, :93, :107, :118-:149 should pass unchanged (water conservation :149 untouched: plenum.js reads E.filterLph only). Recalibrate `F.lph`/`media` of the 6 new rows only if the new curve moves their working point (it will a little: rated 265/505/885 etc.).
11. **Player sees:** Care FilterState: "Its pump (Crane, 1000 L/h, lifts up to 1.4 m) lifts 68 cm and moves 400 L/h"; red note when undersized: "The water has to rise 105 cm: more than this pump can do. It needs a Stork."; gear tile text names the fitted pump. Invented names only.
