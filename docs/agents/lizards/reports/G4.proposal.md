# G4 proposal, step 1 (analysis only): the lizard gait on the real rig (claude-opus-5-5, 10 calls)
1. Module (pure, Node): +src/util/lizardgait.js, a profile row per species (GAIT.gecko now, GAIT.skink later) and functions (API in CONTRACTS.md, "lizardgait API"). From speed v and turn rate w: veff = max(|v|, |w| x foot reach); stride = max(0.45 SVL, veff / fMax), f = veff / stride; phase advances by distance (rate 2pi / stride), so the existing hook at animals.js:953 stays. Gecko, SVL 4.4 cm, game speeds herp.js:99: creep 1.6 -> 0.8 Hz, walk 4.5 -> 2.3 Hz, dash 13 -> 6.6 Hz. Duty 0.65 up to walk, easing to 0.5 at the dash. Leg offsets (cycle) LF 0, RF 0.5, LH 0.43, RH 0.93. Swing: lift-off point to touchdown point on a smooth curve, raised along the normal by 0.04 SVL.
2. Trunk, tail, head: bend = A cos(2pi(phase - LH+RF touchdown)), so convex right at that touchdown, straight at mid-stance, convex left at the other pair's touchdown. Spine joint +-10 deg, neck +-7 deg (together +-17 deg head against pelvis). Head yaw = -(spine + neck), so the head's world yaw stays put (test: residual < 2 deg). Tail yaw = -0.5 x trunk bend, at most 10 deg, lagging 0.1 cycle per bone (passive). Start: the pair whose feet are furthest back swings first. Stop: swings finish, all four feet plant, the wave dies out with `calm` over 0.3 s. Idle: feet planted, axial 0; the head scan and tail tip come from the brain's existing yaw and tail channels.
3. Planting: each lizard inside the skinned distance (`near`) keeps state `a.lg` (Float32Array of 48: per foot the world point, normal, lift-off point and flags; phase). At touchdown the target is the neutral foot (the sheet's sprawl) plus half a stance of predicted travel and turn, projected by a surface callback (ground heightAt, wall zAt, glass plane). That is the only world lookup: 4 per stride. During stance the world point is held and turned into the body frame by the transposed surfaceFrame (9 multiplies). Far lizards: the same targets in closed form, no state.
4. Pose: lizardpose.js `legs` takes `st.feet` (body-frame targets of the digit knuckle, model cm) instead of footOffset (:131). The same fold-and-aim solver aims at the knuckle, then the hand or foot bone is turned flat into the surface plane within its rom. `st.peel[limb]` 0..1 (pad attaches over the first 13 % of stance, peels over the last 36 %) feeds G2's toe channel. Cost per near lizard per frame: about 0.3k flops of gait on top of the existing IK (about 1.5k); far lizards cost nothing new.
5. Surfaces: one code path in the contain.js surfaceFrame body frame (+y = normal) on ground, glass and bark. Belly clearance target is 0-0.05 cm on the ground (< 0.05 body depth) and 0-0.05 cm on wall, bark and glass (belly in contact). Today it is 0.27 and 0.39 cm. The body moves along -n by (rest belly - target) while the feet stay planted, so the limbs splay.
6. Why the feet stand off the wall: wallFrame (animals.js:1143-1162) samples old body points (hlen/hw, not `b.feet` as footing() :4286 does for tailed kinds), so the hind point lies on the tail. It lifts the plane until the HIGHEST of the four points is on the relief (:1157), then adds +0.12 cm (:1161). That +0.12 is exactly the 0.39 - 0.27 belly gap G1c measured. The pose never reaches the feet down either. Fix: a plane fitted (feetPlane) through the planted feet, the belly target, no +0.12. G1c's inGlass guess (:1105) is probably wrong: wallFrame sets pos.z again every frame (inference, not verified).
