# B5d proposal: water below the substrate (step 1, analysis; claude-opus-5-5)

**(a) Modelled today.** False bottom (E.drainage >= 1): plenum.js:117 fits a mesh at E.plenumH = pool+1 cm rounded to 0.5 (:121) over the land footprint (3 cm grid, ground >= H+1 cm, :39); litres = (min(level,H)·0.85 + max(0,level-H)·0.35)·cm²/1000 (:53), e.g. a 6 cm plenum under 2400 cm² holds about 12 L. It fills from rain or misting that soaks down (tau 120 min, :70-73), is pumped from 0.8 cm (:75), seeps to the pool at 2 L/min per cm (:84-88), drains at 3 L/min over a lip at H-0.5 cm (:93-111) and is capped at H+4 (:112). Its share is paid to the pool (:139-143). Writes E.plenumLevel, E.plenumL, E.plenumSoak (:145-146).
LECA (0 < E.drainage < 1; the starter Env uses 0.6, env.js:90; generator.js:558/688 use 0.3/0.25): **nothing in litres.** stepPlenum returns null at :118. The layer's only effect is the drying rate on the per-cell soil index 0…1 (climate.js:187). The renderer draws a fixed 3 cm layer with 1.2 cm of water in it (soilside.js:171,174).
Plain substrate (E.drainage = 0): **nothing below the ground.** Rain and misting only raise the soil index (climate.js:184-191), which is not counted in litres. Nothing is drawn (mode 0, -10 at soilside.js:174).
**(b) Base (step 0).** Add a pure `belowGround(E)` in plenum.js that returns {mode, layerH, level, L}. soilside.update (:168-174) reads only that, never E fields directly, so a node test can show that the renderer's level equals the sim's.
**Part 2, LECA level.** Add a profile argument to `litresAt`/`levelOf`/`plenumStep`; the default is PLENUM, so the B5c test stands. New profile LECA = {h 3 cm, porosity 0.45 (guess: clay-ball voids), soil 0.35, seep 0.3 L/min/cm to the pool (guess), no pump, no drain lip}. It is stepped from stepPlenum's early return (:118), so sim.js is untouched. New state: E.groundLevel (cm over the glass), E.groundL (L), E.groundSoak (L), added to the env.js constructor and Env.KEYS (:92-97), so clearAll/restart reset them. Sources are rain and misting on the land plus seep in from the pool; sinks are seep out and the soil's wicking. Conservation: change in pool + ground = onLand - wick, to 1e-6 L, through the same H.exchange. Drawn: soilside u.water, mode 1, uses the level. Cost per frame: 0.
**Part 3, groundwater with plain substrate.** Profile SOIL = {h 0, porosity 0.35 from the floor (0.84 L per cm under 2400 cm²), seep 0.3 (guess), no drain}. It starts at the pool line, filled with the build (not taken from the pool), or at 0 with no pool. Drawn: mode 0 gets a soaked dark band under the water table with a glint at its line. That is one branch on uniforms, so the shader compiles once at load, then about 5 ALU per strip fragment.
**Part 1, X-ray and Bottom body.** A new `SoilSide.body` mesh in soilside.js covers the footprint grid (pure `footprint(T,H)` in plenum.js, shared with plenumArea; about 450 cells, under 1k triangles in a 90x45 tank). Its top sits at the drawn level through a uniform; it is tea-coloured and translucent and draws through the ground the way the plumbing ghost does (plumbing.js:16,197-202): transparent, after the opaque pass, with no change to the scene pass MRT or samples. It shows only in xray/bottom: layers.js apply() sets W.soilSide.layer and tag() adds the body to `mine` (:52-53). It rebuilds on the soilside signature (groundVer). Cost per frame: 1 draw call and 2 uniform writes, 0 in Surface.
**(c) Largest: part 1** (a new mesh, two views, depth handling, layers.js). Build order: 0 → 2 → 3 → 1. Each part has its own failing node test in tests/belowground.test.mjs.
Files: soilside.js, plenum.js, layers.js, tests/belowground.test.mjs (new), **plus src/sim/env.js** (fields and KEYS). plumbing.js is not needed: the tower line :567 already reads E.plenumLevel, so no lock is needed. sim.js and climate.js are unchanged.
Checks: belowGround(E).level === E.plenumLevel / E.groundLevel for modes 2/1/0 after a seeded day. LECA: the level rises with rain on, then falls back. SOIL: the level rises and falls, and pool + ground conservation holds to 1e-6 L. Body: the top attribute times the level uniform equals belowGround.level (using the pure builder).

## Hand-off
- Decision for the lead (not taken): with a seep open to the pool, an LECA or plain table settles at the pool line. In tanks with a pool deeper than 3 cm, the LECA then shows flooded under the land, which is physically right for soil that slopes into the water. The alternative is to seal the land (only watering raises the level; wicking and a siphon lower it).
- Not in scope (noticed): a high water table does not make the land muddy (climate.js:187-191 and sim.js:139 drainEff). Coupling the two would change gameplay; that is the user's call.
- Trap: plenum.js `area` (:38) is one module-level cache. A second footprint (LECA or SOIL with a different H) needs its own key or it thrashes every step.
- Trap: soilside.js loads three/webgpu, so it cannot run under node. Keep every number the tests check in plenum.js (sim layer); render imports sim, never the reverse.
- Trap: stepPlenum :118 sets hydro.held = 0 for drainage < 1. Leave it as is; the ground store pays the pool only through H.exchange.
- Base tests: `node --test tests/plenum.test.mjs tests/filterflow.test.mjs`. Conservation checks are at plenum.test.mjs:105-121 (the H.total()/all(H,E) pattern; copy it for SOIL) and B5c's one-day bed check at filterflow.test.mjs:163. All must stay green.
