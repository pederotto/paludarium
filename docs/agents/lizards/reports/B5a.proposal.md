# B5a proposal: fish and flow (step 1, analysis only; claude-opus-5-5)
1. Physics: `a.vel` stays the fish's own velocity THROUGH the water (school alignment `b.vel`, steerLimit, maxS, `swimSpeed`/tail beat keep that meaning); swim() adds the water: `pos += (a.vel + w)·dt`, w sampled once per frame. Heading keeps `turnTo(atan2(a.vel))` = swim direction: a fish riding to a downstream goal faces downstream; one holding station faces upstream only because holding IS swimming upstream (emergent, nothing coded). Idle fish: own ~0, drifts.
2. Sampler (new file, no lock): `src/sim/currentat.js` `currentAt(W, x, y, z, out)` = hydro `vx/vz[cellOf]` (d >= 0.3) + `poolCurrent` (jet + intake), times a floor boundary layer `min(1, ((y-floor)/2)^(1/7))` and a lee factor 0.4 when `occ.solidAt` 2 cm upstream (2 probes). Same sum as B5b `plantFlow`, from the sim layer (plantFlow could call it later: noticed).
3. Energy (src/sim/fishmind.js; constants are GUESSES): power `P(u) = 0.15 + (u/Us)^3`, Us = 1.6·sp.speed (sustained), Ub = 2.1·sp.speed (today's dart = burst). Fatigue F in [0,1]: above Us `dF/dt = ((u/Us)^3 - 1)/20` (exhausted after ~15-20 s of burst), below `-(1 - (u/Us)^3)/120` (recovery over minutes). `m.work += P·dt` (the tests' cost). `a.energy = 1 - F`.
4. Tired: F > 0.6: goal = best refuge, cap 0.8·Us, no food darts; F > 0.9: cap 0.5·Us, falls back with the water; label 'Resting in slack water' until F < 0.2. Threat flight may still burst.
5. Leg speed: own = g·ĝ - w, g from {0.3, 0.6, 1, 1.4}·sp.speed minimising `P(|own|)·D/g` with |own| <= cap: downstream goal = low own speed (rides), upstream = high, crossing a jet sideways is cheap.
6. Choices every 0.5 s (staggered by a.phase, seeded, no Math.random): candidates = stay, today's goal (wander/school/band), 3 wet spots <= 10 cm in its band, 1 lee spot (downstream of an occ solid). Score = travel work + hold work `P(|w_spot|)·10 s` (x4 when tired) + `(1 - flow)·max(0, |w| - flow·Us)` - pulls (band, school centre, novelty); 20 % hysteresis.
7. Species: all fish rows have speed/band/school; `flow` only on cpd 0.45 (:379) and pygmy 0.15 (:386), default 0.5 (guess); `flock` is a keeper-sheet count, not behaviour. Hillstream loach: NOT in SPECIES. For it, optional `rheo: { clings, likes }`: clings = on a surface the felt water x0.1 (sucker body in the boundary layer), likes = bonus for |w| near likes·Us (seeks fast water). Cory, clown loach, oto get the floor layer for free.
8. Stays: food dart/nib (overrides the goal; bursts add F), order hunting, schooling sum, wall/occ steering, `filterAvoid` (animals.js:992), B2 `restStep` (R wins; a resting tadpole sits in the floor layer). ctl herps: no mind and no advection in step 2 (open question).
9. Pure API: `fishMind(sp, seed)`; `fishThink(m, s)` -> { goal, own:{x,y,z}, cap, tired, label }, s = { t, dt, x, y, z, w, want, food, flee, probe(x,y,z,out), wet(x,z) } (probe/wet are callbacks, like B2's `danger`); `fishEnergy(m, u, dt)`.
10. Hunk in swim(): ~12 lines added, 3 changed (import; sample w; mind replaces the wander term when !ctl and not resting; cap from m; pos += w·dt; energy). Cost: 1 probe/frame (~0.3 µs) + ~10 probes per 0.5 s; 40 fish ~0.03 ms/frame (guess; measure with a counter like B5b's PF).
11. Checks, failing first (tests/fishflow.test.mjs, pure): idle fish in uniform 5 cm/s: mean drift >= 4 cm/s along it; 30 cm up vs down: work up >= 2x down; tired fish with a slack refuge 8 cm off: there in < 10 s, F down >= 0.3 in 60 s; downstream goal work < still-water work; 40 random-goal fish x 120 s: distance-weighted mean resultant length of (heading - flow dir) < 0.25, mean cos(heading - goal bearing) > 0.8 while moving, downstream legs mean cos(heading, flow) > 0.5 (locked facing gives -1).
12. Tank (state dump): >= 60 % of fish samples where |w| < 0.3·Us; no fish within 1 cm of glass or intake with own speed > 0.8·Us for > 5 s.
